Privacy Policy — CRPS Checklist

Effective date: October 6, 2026
Applies to: CRPS Checklist for iOS

Summary

CRPS Checklist collects nothing. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, or have access to any information about you or your patients. Specifically:

  • No personal information. The app never asks for your name, email address, credentials, or any identifying information, and has no way to receive them.
  • No patient data. Checkbox selections you make while applying the Budapest Criteria are held in the app's memory only. They are never written to storage and are erased when you close the app or switch away from it long enough for iOS to end the session.
  • No analytics or usage tracking. The app contains no analytics SDK, crash reporting service, or usage telemetry.
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers. The app does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and is not part of any advertising network.
  • No device permissions. The app requests no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, health data, or notifications.

Network activity

The app makes no network requests. It functions identically with the device in Airplane Mode. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server, no database, and no third party that receives information from this app.

The Export feature

The app can produce a plain-text summary of the criteria you checked, for pasting into a chart note. When you tap Export, iOS presents its standard share sheet and you choose the destination — Messages, Mail, Notes, the clipboard, or another app. The summary is generated on your device and handed to whichever destination you select.

We never see that text, and we have no involvement in where it goes. Once you send it to another app, that app's own privacy policy governs it. If the summary contains information about a patient, you are responsible for handling it in accordance with HIPAA and your organization's policies.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party software development kits, frameworks, or libraries. It is built entirely on Apple's own iOS frameworks. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

Children

CRPS Checklist is a professional reference tool intended for licensed healthcare providers. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA grant rights to access, correct, delete, and port personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or port. You can remove the app and all of its local state at any time by deleting it from your device.

Medical disclaimer

CRPS Checklist is a clinical decision-support and educational tool. It is not a diagnostic device, has not been cleared or approved by any regulatory body, and does not diagnose, treat, or rule out any condition. It supports clinical judgment and does not replace it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. Because the app collects no data, any change would concern wording rather than practice.
