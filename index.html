CRPS Checklist

Budapest Criteria for complex regional pain syndrome
A clinical decision-support tool for licensed healthcare providers, for iPhone and iPad

About

CRPS Checklist structures the Budapest Criteria — the validated diagnostic standard for complex regional pain syndrome, published by Harden et al. and adopted by the International Association for the Study of Pain — so clinicians can apply them accurately at the bedside. It counts which criteria are satisfied, shows which are not, lists the clinical mimics worth excluding, and explains when and why to refer to pain medicine.

Nothing entered into the app is stored, transmitted, or persisted. It has no account, no analytics, and no network connection.

Important

CRPS Checklist is a clinical decision-support and educational tool. It is not a diagnostic device, has not been cleared or approved by any regulatory body, and does not diagnose, exclude, or treat any condition. CRPS is a clinical diagnosis. This app supports clinical judgment; it does not replace it.
